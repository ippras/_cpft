# Selectivity Factor ($\alpha$)

$$\alpha = \frac{k'_{i + 1}}{k'_i}$$

* $k'_{i + 1} > k'_i$, therefore $\alpha > 1$ is required for separation to take
  place.

The **Selectivity Factor** (also known as the **separation factor**, **relative
retention**, **capacity factor**, **alpha**) measures the thermodynamic
separation between two peaks. It is used to determine how far apart the
retention factors ($k'$) of two components are and whether a separation can be
achieved.
